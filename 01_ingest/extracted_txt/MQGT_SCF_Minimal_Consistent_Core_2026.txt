ABSTRACT
We present a patched, publication-stable "canonical spine" for the MQGT--SCF program.
The framework extends GR+SM by two scalar singlets: a consciousness field Phi_c(x) and an
ethics field E(x). The two central hardening choices are: (i) measurement/collapse is modeled
as a local, covariant completely-positive trace-preserving (CPTP) dynamics in GKSL form
(Tomonaga--Schwinger hypersurface evolution), and (ii) "teleology" is not inserted as a
time-odd source term in the Lagrangian but instead implemented as a Radon--Nikodym/Doob
tilt of the trajectory/outcome measure. We state explicit locality/microcausality conditions
yielding no-signaling, provide an energy-growth bound for collapse noise with finite bandwidth,
give a minimal EFT action with renormalizable couplings, and supply a conservative benchmark
parameter region for phenomenology. Remaining open items are isolated to operationalization of
the ethical functional and numerical constraint-fitting, rather than internal consistency.

1. Patch notes: what was fixed and why
MQGT--SCF drafts sometimes contain multiple co-equal implementations of "teleology" and collapse,
which makes the package feel internally inconsistent. This manuscript chooses one canonical route:

- Teleology: canonical implementation is measure tilt on paths/outcomes (Section 5),
  not an explicit time-arrow term in L.
- Measurement: canonical implementation is local covariant GKSL with microcausality
  (Section 4), ensuring CPTP evolution and blocking superluminal signaling under stated assumptions.

Alternative legacy formulations are quarantined in Appendix A.

2. Field content, symmetries, and EFT action
2.1 Degrees of freedom
The minimal extension adds two real scalar singlets on spacetime (M,g_mu_nu):
Phi_c(x) and E(x),
in addition to GR+SM fields.

2.2 Canonical EFT action
We work as an effective field theory (EFT) with local operators of canonical dimension <= 4.
The action is
S = integral d^4 x sqrt(-g) (L_GR + L_SM + L_Phi_c + L_E + L_int),

with
L_GR = (M_Pl^2/2) R - Lambda,
L_Phi_c = -(1/2)(nabla Phi_c)^2 - V_Phi_c(Phi_c),  V_Phi_c = (1/2) m_Phi_c^2 Phi_c^2 + (lambda_Phi_c/4) Phi_c^4,
L_E = -(1/2)(nabla E)^2 - V_E(E),  V_E = (1/2) m_E^2 E^2 + (lambda_E/4) E^4.

The minimal renormalizable interactions are
L_int = -(kappa/2) Phi_c^2 E^2 - gamma Phi_c E - alpha_H |H|^2 Phi_c - beta_H |H|^2 E
        - g_Phi_c Phi_c O_m - g_E E O_m.

Here H is the SM Higgs doublet and O_m is a coarse-grained matter/neural order parameter.
The bilinear mixing term gamma Phi_c E is treated as a standard interaction, not as a "teleological arrow."

Box Phi_c + m_Phi_c^2 Phi_c + lambda_Phi_c Phi_c^3 + kappa Phi_c E^2 + gamma E = J_Phi_c(x),
Box E + m_E^2 E + lambda_E E^3 + kappa E Phi_c^2 + gamma Phi_c = J_E(x),
where J_Phi_c, J_E encode effective sources from O_m and SM portals.

4. Quantization and measurement: covariant CPTP collapse
4.1 Covariant GKSL in Tomonaga--Schwinger form
Let Sigma be a Cauchy surface and rho[Sigma] a density functional. The canonical
non-unitary dynamics is local GKSL on hypersurfaces.
Microcausality: [L_a(x), L_b(y)] = 0 whenever x and y are spacelike separated.

No-signaling theorem package (v2 assumptions): A1 Local generator, A2 Microcausality,
A3 Linear CPTP dynamics, A4 Spacelike support separation.
Under A1--A4, for any admissible Bob protocol in a spacelike-separated region,
Alice's marginal outcome probabilities are invariant.
